## Pillars

- **Relevant** -- every sentence tells the reader something they act on. Don't restate what's already visible in the code, diff, or context right next to the text.
- **Non-obvious** -- favor what the reader can't infer on their own; skip what they'd already assume.
- **Minimal** -- shortest version that still flows; length follows content, not a quota. In practice: lead with the point instead of warming up to it, keep one idea per sentence, prefer the concrete noun and active verb over abstraction, and cut filler.

## Avoid

- Meta-commentary and arguments against prior versions -- write about the present state only.
- Hype words ("seamless", "powerful", "genuinely", "robust") unless they carry real information.
